## ⚡ Who I am

Self-taught **embedded systems engineer, full-stack developer, researcher and founder**. I've been building since ~2015, and I take ideas all the way from a bare PCB to a deployed product: **hardware → firmware → backend → web/mobile app → investor materials**.

I build practical technology for **African and emerging-market problems**: healthcare, environmental monitoring, infrastructure, automation and access to digital services.

- 🏢 Founder, **Andie Labs**, an engineering and R&D company for embedded, IoT, robotics and software products
- 🩺 Co-founder & lead builder, **Brykel Health**, vital-signs monitoring tech for rural and remote hospitals
- 🎓 Studying BSc Computer Science @ University of the People, while shipping
- ⚽ Arsenal till the end
